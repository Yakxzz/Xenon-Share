A simple three-step idea

From your Drive
to someone you trust.

No account maze for the person receiving your memories. The planned experience is built around one small code and the moments you choose to share.

STEP 01 — CONNECT

Connect your Drive

Choose Google Drive as the place your photos and videos already live. You stay in control of what you select.

STEP 02 — CREATE

Create a six-digit code

Generate a simple code for the set of memories you want to share, instead of sending a pile of individual files.

STEP 03 — VIEW

They enter. They view.

The recipient enters your code and views the memories you chose to make available. No need to forward the original files around.

The principle behind Xenon

Your memories aren't our content.

Xenon Share is being designed around a clear boundary: your photos and videos should remain yours. The intended architecture keeps your media in Google Drive and avoids building a Xenon-owned library of people's memories.

Important: Xenon Share is not live yet. This is the privacy goal for the product, not a verified security guarantee. The final Google Drive permissions, access flow, and technical implementation must be tested before launch.
No Xenon media libraryThe product is intended to avoid storing copies of your memories on Xenon servers.
No training or reuseYour personal photos and videos are not meant to become product content or AI training material.
You choose what to shareThe intended flow starts with the memories you select and the person you choose to share them with.
